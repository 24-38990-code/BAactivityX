The Rise of Sung Jinwoo

Solo Leveling follows Sung Jinwoo, a hunter who begins his journey as one of the weakest members of humanity's hunter community.

After receiving a mysterious System, Jinwoo gains the ability to become stronger by completing quests and defeating enemies. His transformation changes his life and places him at the center of a much larger conflict.
